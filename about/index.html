About shelfstackd

  • shelfstackd is a shelf for the films and books you love.
  • Type a title and it finds the real spine, from a DVD case or a book.
  • Your shelf goes on your profile, and out to your story in one press.
  • Log what you watch and read; its cover wears a little with time, as yours would.
  • Follow your friends, and send them something for their Up next.
