            How it works

            1. You type a name. Films come from TMDB, books from Open Library.
            2. We look for full DVD and book scans of that title.
            3. The strip between back and front is the spine. We cut it out.
            4. No clean scan? You get a spine made from the poster or cover.

            Sources

            Film data from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Book data from Open Library. Scans belong to their studios and publishers, and every spine links back to where it was found.
