ambientCG's licence: "All ambientCG assets are provided under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal

Each Commons file's licence was checked on its file page (the licence template in the page's wikitext),
not only in search results.

How they were made: square crop, greyscale, contrast stretched to the full range, JPEG at 65–80 %,
under 200 KB each. ambientCG maps already tile, so they're otherwise unchanged (Wood095 keeps its
1024×512 plank shape). The two Commons photos were made to tile by blending each with a half-shifted
copy of itself.
